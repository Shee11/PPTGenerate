## Overview

Widget style presets allow you to customize the visual appearance of widgets through 4 categories with 22 variants total.

## Categories

- **Surface** (6): Flat, Elevated, Outline, Glass, Sunken, NeoBrutal
- **Shape** (6): Sharp, Rounded, Curve, Pill, Squircle, Organic
- **Fill** (6): Solid_Brand, Subtle, Gradient_Linear, Gradient_Mesh, Pattern_Dot, Noise
- **Effect** (4): Duotone, Glitch, Glow, Tape

See [data-model.md](data-model.md) for complete variant definitions and CSS properties.
