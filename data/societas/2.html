The Ancient Foundation: Text-Only Era

Early Human Civilization Meetings

Written language was the sole medium available for recording meeting outcomes and decisions.
Text-based documentation represented the only possible output format from collaborative gatherings.
Human civilization relied entirely on verbal discussion followed by manual transcription processes.
No multimedia formats existed to express complex ideas beyond written words and basic drawings.
Historical Context: The foundation of all meeting practices was established when text was humanity's primary tool for preserving and sharing knowledge across time and distance.
