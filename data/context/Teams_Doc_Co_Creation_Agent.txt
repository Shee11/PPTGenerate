Slide 2: The Vision – Spoken Intent to Immediate Output
•	Header: The Evolution of Meeting Outputs.
•	Left (Past): Written language was the sole medium, resulting in "Text Only" output.
•	Middle (Present): Human bottleneck in generating tool-ready outputs (The Productivity Gap).
•	Right (Future): Active, Real-time Generator where AI bridges the gap by turning live conversation into structured artifacts.
•	Key Message: We are moving Teams from a communication tool to a "Productivity Studio" and "Central Hub".
________________________________________
Slide 3: Sharpening the Unique Value of Teams
•	Differentiation: Why build in Teams vs. PPT or M365 Copilot?.
•	The "Freshness" Advantage: We have immediate access to fresh transcripts and multi-modal grounding (chat, shared files, and live sentiment).
•	Data Gravity: Creating docs immediately within the meeting ecosystem enriches our data lake and drives higher user retention.
•	Workflow Integration: Teams is where users start their workflow; we capture intent at the point of origin rather than asking them to context-switch later.
________________________________________
Slide 4: Strategic Alignment – The Drafting & Editing Pipeline
•	Strategic Frame: Teams is the Incubation Point; PowerPoint is the Refinement Studio.
•	The Teams Agent Role: Focuses on capturing context, mining "Atoms" (facts/metrics), and creating the initial structural draft.
•	The PPT Team Role: Provides the "Last Mile"—professional design polish, layout intelligence, and final high-fidelity editing.
•	Synergy: We feed the PPT ecosystem by eliminating the "blank slide" problem for 30M files created daily.
________________________________________
Slide 5: User Journey – The "Slides Studio" Experience
•	Step 1: Contextual Capture: The Agent identifies key data points (e.g., "Revenue Target: $50M") from the live transcript.
•	Step 2: Intention Queue: Users see an "Intention Queue" in the sidebar to approve or reject suggested slide topics in real-time.
•	Step 3: Storyline View: A structured narrative view (Storyline) allows users to see the flow before the pixels are placed.
•	Step 4: Real-Time Preview: High-fidelity slide previews appear immediately, allowing for collaborative review without leaving the call.
________________________________________
Slide 6: Technical Feasibility & Solving the "WOW" Factor
•	Architecture: A modular engine consisting of a Miner (Facts), Weaver (Narration), and Assembler (Layout & Style)14.

•	Core Metrics:
o	Time-to-Artifact: Reduction in hours from "Meeting End" to "First Draft."
