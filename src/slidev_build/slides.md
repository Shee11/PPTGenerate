## The Old AI Game: Slow, Siloed, Expensive

::beforeLabel::

### Traditional Workflow

::before::

- Linear pipeline: data → model → training → deployment
- Different owners for each stage, handoffs everywhere
- Months of data collection and tuning before users see anything
- Teams repeatedly reinvented infra, evaluation, and optimization

::afterLabel::

### Hidden Cost

::after::

- Huge upfront investment before real feedback
- Optimized models… but mediocre products
- Every new project felt like starting from zero

::left::

## LLMs Changed the Center of Gravity

::right::

- Large models act like a **unified knowledge base with a reasoning interface**.
- We no longer start by training a model from scratch.
- We start by **composing**: prompts, tools, data, and workflows.
- Optimization moved from squeezing 0.1% accuracy →
crafting better UX, faster iteration loops, and smarter orchestration.

::title::

## The 3 High-ROI Superpowers in the LLM Era

::box1_title::

#### 1. Precise Problem Definition

::box1_content::

Think beyond today’s model limits.
Define problems at the **product and system level**:
• What outcome do we really want?
• What data and feedback can we loop back in?
• How could this be 10x more useful, not just 1% more accurate?

::box2_title::

#### 2. Robust Evaluation & Tuning

::box2_content::

Move from one-off benchmarks to **living evaluation systems**:
• Telemetry, edge cases, and failure pattern tracking
• Iterative prompt tuning instead of magic prompts
• Automated feedback loops that refine behavior over time

::box3_title::

#### 3. Cross-Domain Combination

::box3_content::

Depth in one stack is no longer enough.
Value comes from combining **multiple tools and domains**:
• LLMs + search + structured data
• Code + product sense + UX
• Turning messy real-world problems into orchestrated systems

## 1

**Skill AI can’t replace (yet)**

Defining the right problem at the right scope

::right::

- LLMs will out-code and out-optimize us in many local tasks.
- What they still lack is **judgment about scope**: what truly matters at the system and product level.
- Your edge:
  • Make every step solid and understandable.
  • Choose problems where human insight multiplies AI power.
  • Evolve with the tools instead of competing against them.
- **Don’t just build with AI.
Redefine what’s worth building.**
