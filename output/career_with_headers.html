Context: the AI journey

Career inflection points

A tech career can progress through inflection points such as moving from computer vision to deep learning, then to cloud services, and later to large language models and AI agents.

Inflection points create chances to re-level skills, not to restart

Lesson 1: start with applied ML

Computer vision builds applied machine learning skills that become a foundation for later transitions into other AI domains.

Start: vision

Working in computer vision builds applied machine learning skills that transfer to future AI work.

Moving from traditional computer vision to deep learning is a natural evolution as AI techniques advance.

Career transitions between domains are enabled by building on prior skills rather than starting from scratch.

Focus: applied ML fundamentals, not specific libraries or toolchains

Lesson 2: think beyond the model

From models to systems

Transitioning from model-focused work to cloud services exposes engineers to distributed systems and scalability concerns.

Understanding distributed systems and scalability is a key skill for cloud-based AI products.

A combination of applied ML and distributed systems skills becomes increasingly valuable in modern software development.

Add system design, scalability, and reliability to the ML toolkit

Climax: LLMs reshape software

Working with large language models and AI agents is a current frontier that is changing how software is developed.

A combination of applied machine learning, distributed systems, scalability, and LLM or agent skills is increasingly valuable in modern software development. LLMs and agents reshape how products are designed, how features are shipped, and how engineers interact with code and infrastructure.

Career leverage now sits at the intersection of ML, systems, and LLMs or agents

How to choose the next domain

Career transitions between domains are enabled by building on prior skills rather than starting from scratch.

Observe emerging technologies and industry shifts such as deep learning, cloud services, or large language models.

Identify emerging areas that connect most strongly to existing skills, such as moving from computer vision to deep learning.

Apply new skills in practical projects that integrate previous domain knowledge with new technologies.

Use each transition to compound skills instead of resetting your stack

Process: choosing what to learn next

A repeatable transition loop

  • Observe emerging technologies and industry shifts such as deep learning, cloud services, or large language models.
  • Prioritize technologies that help solve real problems faced by you or your users.
  • Systematically learn foundational concepts such as distributed systems or prompt design and evaluation.
  • Apply new skills in projects that integrate previous domain knowledge with new technologies.
  • Repeat this cycle of observing shifts, learning, and applying to stay aligned with industry direction.

Treat learning as a continuous loop, not a one-time decision
