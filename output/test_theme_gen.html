Growing Your Career in the LLM Era

AI careers are shifting

From building models to orchestrating intelligent systems

Overview · Slide 1

Lesson 1 · Evolving AI Roles

Lesson 1: Roles are blending; focus on orchestration and reliability

Shift: from linear pipelines and fixed roles to composing large models into applications.

High-ROI focus: user experience, reliability, and iteration speed over tiny accuracy gains.

Engineer vs applied scientist now differ more by focus than by responsibilities.

Action: practice designing agent architectures, prompts, and failure-handling paths, not just models.

Key Lessons · Slide 2

Lesson 2 · High-ROI Thinking Skills

Lesson 2: Invest in problem definition and cross-domain thinking

LLMs remove many component limits; problem framing no longer has to follow existing modules.

High value: combine multiple domains to design more useful, realistic products and workflows.

Practice: imagine better products first, then work backward to what data, tools, and models are needed.

Remember: defining the right scope is a human strength that current LLMs do not match yet.

Key Lessons · Slide 3

Lesson 3 · Feedback and Learning Systems

Lesson 3: Build feedback loops, evaluation, and solid learning habits

High-ROI work: design robust evaluation and automated tuning to accelerate iteration and catch edge cases.

Aim for systems with telemetry and adaptive loops, not just one-off model accuracy improvements.

Learning habit: treat each question–answer as a durable asset; do not move on until it truly makes sense.

Practice: make each piece of code or design solid, then record what you learned for reuse and growth.
